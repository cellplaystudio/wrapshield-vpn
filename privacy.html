WrapShield VPN — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026 App: WrapShield VPN (Android, package com.cellplaystudio.wrapshieldvpn) Developer and data controller: CellPlay Studio Contact: cellplaystudio@gmail.com

This policy explains, in plain words, what information WrapShield VPN handles, why, who else is involved, and what choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses the app, wherever you live, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States (including California and other states with privacy laws), Brazil, India and all other regions. It is part of our Terms of Use.

1. The short version

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not record the websites you visit, your DNS requests, the apps you use, your traffic, or your IP address.
  • We run no servers that receive your traffic or your data. Your traffic is sent through the Tor network, which is operated by independent volunteers.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking code of our own.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google handles the data needed to show and measure ads (section 8). Where the law requires it, we ask for your choice first. Pro removes all ads.
  • Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising ourselves.
  • The app is for people aged 18 and over.

2. Who is responsible

CellPlay Studio ("we", "us") publishes WrapShield VPN. Because the app sends no personal data to us, we usually hold no personal data about you. Google (for ads and purchases) and the Tor network (for routing) are separate parties that run their own services under their own policies; they are explained below.

3. What the app does

WrapShield VPN uses Android's VPN feature (VpnService) to send your phone's internet traffic, or only the apps you choose, through the Tor network. This hides what you do online from the owner of your Wi-Fi and from your mobile provider, and hides your location from the websites you visit. Android asks for your permission before any VPN can start. The app does not read, copy, store or send the content of your traffic to us.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or receive: your browsing history, the websites you visit, DNS queries, the content of your traffic, your IP address, connection times or session logs, your contacts, messages, call logs, photos, files, microphone or camera data, precise location, or an account or profile of you.

5. Information kept only on your phone

These items are kept in the app's private storage on your phone and are never sent to us:

  • your settings (dark or light mode, chosen exit country, Fast mode);
  • your choices for split tunneling (which apps use the VPN) and, if you use the optional proxy, the proxy address, port and login;
  • a simple "Pro: yes or no" flag and your answer to the in-app VPN explanation (agree or decline);
  • which way of connecting worked best on your current type of network (Wi-Fi or mobile data), so the next connection is faster;
  • a counter of how many times the app was opened and how many connections you made, used only to decide when to show the Pro offer, and a note of whether you already rated the app and how often the rating reminder was shown (it is shown at most twice, and never again once you have rated).

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of this data are switched off. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it. The app can list the launcher apps installed on your phone so you can choose them for split tunneling; this list is used on your phone only and is never sent anywhere.

6. How your traffic travels (Tor and helper connections)

  • Tor: your traffic is sent through several volunteer-run servers (relays) around the world. No single relay knows both who you are and what you are doing. The last relay (the "exit") connects to the website you asked for and can see traffic that is not encrypted, such as plain http:// websites. Use https:// websites. The Tor Project's own information is at https://www.torproject.org.
  • Helper connections (bridges): on networks that block privacy apps, the app may ask the Tor Project (bridges.torproject.org) for "bridge" helper connections, and may use services such as Snowflake to get through. These services can see your IP address, as any website or service you connect to can. We do not control them.
  • Optional proxy: if you enter a proxy, your connection is sent through it. The proxy owner can see that you connect to Tor. Only enter a proxy you trust.
  • Built-in checks: when you use the tools in the app, it makes small requests through Tor: a connection check to a Google connectivity address, an address check to the Tor Project's address service (check.torproject.org), and, only if you tap "Speed test", a download from public test servers. Those services see an address of a Tor exit relay, not yours, and no personal data.
  • No guarantee of anonymity: no tool can promise complete anonymity. Logging in to an account, sharing personal details, or using a website that tracks you can still identify you.

7. Permissions the app uses

  • VPN (Android's VPN permission): to send traffic through Tor. You grant this in an Android dialog.
  • Internet and network state: to connect and to notice when you switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  • Google Play Billing and advertising ID: for the Pro purchase and, in the free version, ads (section 8).
  • Wake lock and foreground service (added by software libraries): to keep the connection working.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, SMS or phone permissions.

8. Ads (free version only)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use, from your device: the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information (such as model, operating system and language), and ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks). This data is used for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention and, where you consent, personalization. Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • When ads load: the app asks for new ads only while the VPN is off. The app's own connection to Google's ad service uses your normal internet connection, not Tor. An ad that was loaded earlier may still be visible while you are connected. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and above, and ad requests are marked as not child-directed.
  • Your choices:
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we use Google's consent message (a certified consent management platform). No ad is requested until you have made your choice. You can change it at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. If you decline personalised ads you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • In US states with privacy laws, the same Ad privacy choices entry in Settings (where it is offered) lets you opt out of personalised ads and of the sharing of data for advertising.
  • Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Pro removes all ads and stops ad requests.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Data collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google's own terms and policies.

9. Purchases

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes your payment and keeps the purchase record. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro and stores a simple "Pro: yes or no" flag on your phone. We never receive your name, card or payment details. Use Restore purchase on the Pro screen if you reinstall the app or change phones.

10. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Showing ads and using the advertising ID: your consent, collected through Google's consent message. You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 8.
  • Running the VPN function on your device: performance of the service you asked for and our legitimate interest in providing a working app. No personal data is sent to us for this.
  • Pro purchase: performance of a contract, handled by Google Play.

11. Sharing and international transfers

We do not share personal data with anyone, because we do not hold any. Google (ads, Play Billing) and the Tor network run independently and may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards (including standard contractual clauses where they apply). We do not use service providers, data brokers or analytics partners.

We keep no personal data about you. Local settings stay on your phone until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. To delete data held by Google (advertising ID, ad profile, purchase history), use your Google account and Android settings, or contact Google.

14. Security

Data between your device and Google's ad and billing services is encrypted in transit (TLS). Traffic sent through the VPN is encrypted between your phone and the Tor exit relay. The app has no user database and no server to attack, and it is open source (GPL-3.0), so anyone can check what it does. No system is perfectly secure; keep your phone updated. To report a security problem, email cellplaystudio@gmail.com with the subject "Security".

15. Law enforcement and legal requests

We do not hold traffic logs, browsing history, connection records or account data, so we cannot provide them. If we receive a lawful request, we will tell the requester what we have just said, unless the law prevents us.
